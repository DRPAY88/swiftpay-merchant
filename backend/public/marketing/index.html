KRW COLLECTION · A CLEAR GUIDE

Pay in won.
Merchant receives PHP.

When a DRPAY merchant offers KRW collection, scan their QR with Kakao, Toss, or KBank and pay in Korean won.

  1. The merchant checks a live KRW-to-PHP quote and creates your payment QR.
  2. Scan the QR in Kakao, Toss, or KBank and pay in KRW.
  3. After the provider verifies your payment, PHP is credited to the merchant's balance.

For merchants: KRW collection requires an eligible account with multicurrency enabled and a current KRW-to-PHP rate configured.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
download.

Can I exchange KRW directly to PHP or USDT on TRC20 today?

Not yet. Direct KRW-to-PHP or KRW-to-USDT exchange is planned, not live. Today, DRPAY offers KRW-priced QR payment quotes and separate PHP/USDT checkout features.

Will I receive PHP or USDT instantly?

Fast payout is the goal, but exchange is not available yet. Rates, checks, and provider processing may affect payout time.

Which devices can install DRPAY?

DRPAY runs on Android 7.0 and newer.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK, open it, and follow Android's installation prompts.

How do I create an account?

Open the Android app, enter your email, and verify the one-time code. Your account is activated automatically—no KYB application or document upload.

Does every account have its own wallet?

Yes. Each verified account has its own wallet balance and activity, starting at zero. Top-ups are credited only after confirmed settlement. The wallet is an app-managed ledger over pooled provider funds, not a separate bank account.
